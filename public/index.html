Tournament

Generate a group stage and/or knockout bracket from an event's registrations (or from teams you group them into), run it through to a winner, and optionally feed the final standings straight into that event's points.

Delete an event

Works for both upcoming and already-finished events. Deleting an event also removes every registration tied to it - this can't be undone. A parent event with activities nested under it can't be deleted until those activities are deleted or reassigned first.
